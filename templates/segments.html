Customers with moderate income and high spending behavior.

Cluster 2

Average Customers

Customers showing balanced income and spending patterns.

Strategy: Cross-selling opportunities
🎯

Cluster 3

Low Spending Customers

Customers with limited spending activity.

Strategy: Discounts & awareness campaigns
👑

Cluster 4

Loyal Customers

High-value customers with strong purchasing behavior.
